Who We Are?

InAmigos Foundation is a community-driven non-profit organisation founded on 23rd September, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO) with one belief: compassion in action can transform lives.

From slum children who need education to stray animals who need food, from women seeking self-reliance to youth seeking skills — we work where the need is real and the impact is visible.

In four years, the foundation reports distributing 50,000+ meals, planting 20,000+ trees, feeding 50+ animals daily, empowering 900+ women and training 30,000+ interns.

With a network of professionals, volunteers and corporate partners, we work to bring positive change in society while keeping supporters informed through social media.

At InAmigos Foundation, we believe in the power of collective action. Contributions support causes such as food distribution, education, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental sustainability and social inclusion.
