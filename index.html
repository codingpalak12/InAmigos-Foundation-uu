Who We Are ?


InAmigos Foundation is a community driven non-profit organisation founded on 23rd September, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO) with one belief : Compassion in action can transform lives. It has its base at chandigarh

From slum children who need education to stray animals who need food, from women seeking self-reliance to youth seeking skills - we work where the need is real and the impact is visible.

In just 4 years, we have distributed 50,000+ meals, planted 20,000+ trees, fed 50+ animals daily, empowered 900+ women and trained 30,000+ interns.

With a strong network of dedicated professinals, volunteers and corporate partners, we work tirelessly to bring positive change in society. Our social media platforms actively showcase our impact, keeping our supporters informed and engaged.

At InAmigos Foundation, webelieve in power of collective action. Every contribution is directed towards essential causes such as food distribution, education, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental sustainability and social inclusion programs across India.
